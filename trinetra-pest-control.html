Frequently asked questions

Is the treatment safe for kids and pets?

Yes — we use government-approved products applied strictly as per label safety guidance, and advise simple precautions like keeping the area clear for a few hours after treatment.

How long does a visit usually take?

Most home visits take 30–60 minutes depending on property size and the pest being treated.
